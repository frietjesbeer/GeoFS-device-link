This is a 2 in 1 addon for GeoFS that links your sim to a website, what does it do?

A primary flight display linked to GeoFS which you can display on any device with a web browser. This way you have a more clear view of your speed, altitude etc, which can get blurry if you zoom out enough in cockpit view.


<img width="1672" height="941" alt="Pause Sim at Sunset" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/00b9bc23-2e58-4d55-98f5-d3680fd1082c" />

An autopilot panel with a pause button so you can control your aircraft, even when you're not at home. Combine this with radarthing: https://github.com/mansoorbarri/RadarThing and you always know where your aircraft is and what to do.


If your phone has a gyro (almost every smartphone has one), the website will auto switch to the PFD when you turn your phone to horizontal mode.


How to install?
- Install geofslink.js in the console, or via tampermonkey.
- Open the website https://frietjesbeer.github.io/GeoFS-device-link/ on the device you want your PFD / AP panel on.
- When opening GeoFs a small plane shaped icon will appear in the bottom bar, click that and you will see a dashboard.
- On the website, go to the autopilot panel and click on the settings icon at the top right of your screen.
- Copy the peer code and the token and paste them into the dashboard in GeoFS.
- GeoFS and the website will automatically link to each other and you're good to go!
